# Session Script

The following script records the session procedure and my wording. Recordings were made locally in Zoom and transcribed later on my laptop.

| Step | What happens | What I say |
|---|---|---|
| P number | Take the next unused number, P1 to P6. The interface knows the rest. |  |
| Zoom | Start a new meeting, camera off, do not record yet. Send the study interface through the chat. | “I’ve just sent you a link in the chat. Could you open it in your browser? Nothing installs. Then please share your whole screen.” |
| Setup screen | Pick the P number, click Begin session. |  |
| Consent | They read; I stay quiet. | “Two of those boxes are separate choices, not part of agreeing to take part. One is whether I can record the audio, and one is whether I can quote you anonymously. You can say no to either and still do the study.” Then: “The first two background questions are needed to take part. You can skip the other background questions and any of the questions at the end. The four main tasks need a decision, a confidence number and a line of explanation before the screen moves on. If you hit one you'd rather not answer, tell me, and we stop.” |
| Recording | Only if the recording box is ticked. | “I'm starting the recording now.” |
| Background | Four questions. If screening is not met, thank them and end. | “A few quick questions about your background first.” |
| Briefing | They read the situation screen. | “Same idea for every case: you're checking a number before it goes into your labmate's draft. The most important thing for me is that you think aloud the whole time. If you go quiet, I'll say, ‘What are you thinking?’, and that's only a reminder.” “I'm not scoring you, and I won't tell you whether you got anything right, because there often isn't one right answer.” |
| Warm-up | Four-step tour, then the warm-up case. I answer questions only about the screen. | After it: “In your own words, what is this number actually measuring?” and “What would make you pick ‘fix one thing first’ rather than ‘not yet, something needs checking’?” Correct if wrong, note it. |
| Four cases | Silent except the fixed prompts. | Quiet 15 seconds: “What are you thinking?” Trailing off: “Say a bit more about that?” “Is this wrong?”: “What do you think?” “Should I…?”: “Whatever makes sense to you.” About the yellow box: “Whatever it means to you.” “Is there a right answer?”: “Not one I'm scoring.” Never “good”, “right”, “exactly”, “hmm”. |
| Closing questions | They read and type on screen. | “That's the cases done. The last part is six short questions on the screen. Just type your answers.” |
| Debrief | Read aloud from the screen.  | The debrief text on the Questions tab. |
| Download | They click Download session file and send it through the chat. | “Could you send me the file that downloads through the Zoom chat?” |
| Same day | Move the recording to the participant's folder. |  |
